Data sources and methods: what each sensor and prediction is

Sources: USGS Water Services, NOAA CO-OPS, NOAA NDBC via IOOS, National Weather Service. Data are provisional. USGS velocities are cross-section means, not the surface current in the channel. Water levels are in MLLW (chart datum, 1983–2001 epoch). Fort Pulaski is NOAA's published datum; Elba Island, Savannah, Garden City and Port Wentworth use offsets computed for this page from each gauge's record with NOAA's Tidal Analysis Datum Calculator, and are not official NOAA datums. I-95 is in NAVD88. Future times show the experimental forecast of water level and current at the gauges (marked "forecast", with its 95% range), tide and tidal current predictions where there is no forecast (marked "predicted"), and the NWS wind forecast; transit distances are approximate. Tide predictions at the USGS gauges, and the section-mean current predictions at Fort Pulaski, Savannah and Port Wentworth, are harmonic predictions computed for this page from each gauge's 2021–2026 record; they are not official NOAA predictions and do not include changes in river flow or weather.
